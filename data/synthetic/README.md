# Synthetic regression fixtures

These fabricated records exercise the retained legacy analysis pipeline. They are required by automated tests and are not learner observations.

`scripts/make-synthetic-fixtures.mjs` reproduces them. SYNTH-A contains a complete prepared attempt with 4/6 term and 3/6 meaning scores; SYNTH-B contains 2/6 approved cues at a preparation cap and no recall.

Do not present their scores as evidence for Classroom Detective, Planetarium Rescue or Earth Explorer.
