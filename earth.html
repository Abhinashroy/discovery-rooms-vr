How to play

Orbit: drag the scene to orbit the model; scroll to zoom. Time opens the date and 24-hour controls.

Globe: WASD / Up & Down travel, drag look, Left & Right turn, E selects ahead. You can cross oceans on the learning surface. Countries offers searchable country destinations and cards.

Quest: left stick takes short steps with fades; right stick snap-turns. Left trigger on Earth teleports, right trigger opens country information. B/Y toggles controls. Country destinations support seated play.

You are a giant traveller: the globe is smaller and the eye viewpoint is raised. The gold locator ring identifies small countries without enlarging their borders. Stars and occasional distant comets are decorative. Sound can be muted.

Switching views preserves discoveries and time. Main room ends this run.

Map, science & credits

Approximate teaching model: orbital timing, tilt and sunlight share one clock. Bodies, distances and walking scale are adjusted; this is not an eclipse or sunrise prediction tool.

Country cards use world regions, including Oceania for Australia and Pacific islands. Countries spanning Europe and Asia have a combined label and paired-continent quiz questions. Antarctica has no sovereign countries. Natural Earth 50m boundaries use its de facto policy, omit small features and are not a resolution of territorial claims.

Textures: Solar System Scope / INOVE, CC BY 4.0. Country facts: original summaries of UNESCO, government, tourism and educational sources.
