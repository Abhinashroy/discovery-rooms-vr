Solar system learning notes

The Sun is a star. Eight planets orbit it; Pluto is a dwarf planet. To orbit means to travel around another body. Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are rocky planets. Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants; Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.
