## Country geometry

Country polygons are derived from **Natural Earth v5.1.2, 1:50 million admin-0 countries**, public-domain data. Unused metadata is removed; rings and holes are retained. Boundary policy, identifiers and hashes are in the Earth Explorer manifest. Outlines are generalized map data, not a statement resolving territorial claims.

Made with Natural Earth: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>.

## Learning content and audio

Country facts are original factual summaries with per-card references, primarily UNESCO and official tourism, government, museum and educational sources. Source photographs, logos and descriptive passages are not reproduced. Planetarium questions also retain their supporting references in source.

Audio clips are user-supplied project recordings. `public/audio/footstep.wav` is a single thud extracted from `sounds/walk sound.mp3`; the extraction helper preserves the trim and fades.

No project-wide open-source license has been selected. Public repository visibility does not replace the licenses and attribution requirements of these bundled components.
